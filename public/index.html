📜 How to Play — Official Tournament Rules
  1. Table Capacity: Exactly 4 players required.
  2. Hand Distribution: Each player gets 13 cards from a standard 52-card deck.
  3. Rank Hierarchy: Ace (highest) > K > Q > J > 10 > 9 > 8 > 7 > 6 > 5 > 4 > 3 > 2.
  4. Suit Domains: Spades (♠), Hearts (♥), Diamonds (♦), Clubs (♣).
  5. Opening Gambit: The player with the Ace of Spades (A♠) leads Round 1.
  6. Elimination Goal: First player to discard all cards wins Rank 1.
  7. Following Suit: You must play a card matching the lead suit if you have one.
  8. Trick Clearing: If everyone follows suit, cards are discarded and the highest card holder leads next.
  9. The Rule of Thullu: If you can't follow suit, play any card. The highest lead-suit card holder picks up ALL table cards!
  10. Rule 10 (Last Stand): If you empty your hand but lead the next trick, you draw a random card from the player with the most cards.
  11. 15-Second Blitz: You have 15 seconds per move. Timer expiry auto-plays a legal card.
  12. First Round Grace: Thullu in Round 1 discards table cards safely (no pickup). Ace of Spades holder leads Round 2.
